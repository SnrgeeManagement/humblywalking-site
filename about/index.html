Our Story

How it all started

01

Founded Through Faith

Humbly Walking With God® was born in 2023 after a 4:00 a.m. prayer meeting, where God transformed a simple idea into a clear vision. What began as uncertainty became a calling rooted in faith and obedience.

02

A Mother-Daughter Vision

Inspired by the spiritual journeys of a mother and daughter, our brand is built on the belief that true success is measured by how humbly we walk with God, no matter where life takes us.

03

From Setback to Purpose

After launching previous businesses, facing discouragement, and being laid off from her job, founder Ariel Jones chose to trust God's timing. What looked like a setback became the foundation for a greater purpose.

04

More Than Apparel

Humbly Walking With God® exists to create intentional clothing that serves as a daily reminder to pursue humility, live faithfully, and reflect Christ in every season of life.

05

Excellence in Every Detail

We are committed to providing thoughtfully designed, high-quality apparel and a premium shopping experience that reflects the excellence and intentionality behind our mission.
