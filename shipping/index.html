Customer Care · Humbly Walking, LLC

Shipping Policy

At Humbly Walking with God®, every piece is created with intentionality, prayer, and quality craftsmanship. We want your order to arrive safely and on time, and we're grateful you've chosen to walk with us.

All sales are final. While we don't offer refunds or general returns, we're committed to making things right if your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 4–5 business days (Monday through Friday, excluding holidays). Processing time is separate from shipping time and begins once your payment has been confirmed.

Shipping Method

All orders ship via USPS or UPS Ground. The carrier is selected based on your location and package size to get your order to you as efficiently as possible.

Shipping Fees

Shipping rates vary based on your order size, weight, and destination. Your exact shipping cost will be calculated and shown at checkout before you complete your purchase.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order has shipped, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with your tracking number so you can follow your package every step of the way.

Shipping Restrictions

We currently ship within the United States only. We are unable to ship to international addresses at this time.

Delays

Most orders arrive within the expected time frame, but occasional delays can happen due to high order volume, weather, holidays, or carrier disruptions. Once your package is in the carrier's hands, delivery times are outside of our control. We appreciate your patience and grace, and we'll always do our best to help if your order is delayed.

Address Accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address before placing your order. Humbly Walking with God® is not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect or incomplete address provided at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us at info@humblywalking.com as soon as possible. We'll do our best to update it, but once an order has shipped we can no longer make changes. Packages returned to us due to an incorrect address may require additional shipping fees to resend.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, we'll make it right with a replacement. Please email info@humblywalking.com within 7 days of delivery and include:

  • Your order number
  • Clear photos of the defective or incorrect item (and the packaging, if it was damaged in transit)
  • A brief description of the issue

Please use the subject line: Replacement Request – [Your Order Number]

Approval and Shipment

Once we receive your request, our team will review it and respond within 2–3 business days. If your replacement is approved, we'll ship your new item at no additional cost, and you'll receive a confirmation email with your tracking number once it's on the way. Replacements are subject to availability; if the same item is no longer available, we'll work with you on a comparable option. Requests submitted after 7 days of delivery, or for items that show signs of wear, washing, or misuse, may not be eligible.

Questions

Have a question about your order or our shipping policy? Email us at info@humblywalking.com and we'll be happy to help. Our response hours are Monday – Friday, 10AM – 6PM CST.
